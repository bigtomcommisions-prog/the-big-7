PROJECT 01

Hearthvale

Your Discord server, as a cosy little world you can walk around in.

Live · free to play in your browser
{{icon:Gamepad2}} Play Hearthvale {{icon:Users}} Join the dev server

{{icon:Trees}} Towns from channels

Categories become towns and text channels become houses. It all updates live as the server changes.

{{icon:MessageCircle}} Chat into Discord

Step inside a house to read the channel and post real messages as yourself.

{{icon:Mic}} Proximity voice

Hear players nearby, muffled through walls, with push-to-talk and mute.

{{icon:Headphones}} Talk to Discord calls

Walk into a voice gazebo to join the people in that Discord voice channel.

{{icon:Shirt}} Your villager

Pick a body, hair, face and up to six accessories. Your look is saved.

{{icon:ShieldCheck}} Respects permissions

You only see, post and talk where Discord allows you to.

Log in with Discord to play. See the Hearthvale privacy section and the Terms of Service. Not affiliated with Discord.

PROJECT 02

OmniPrice

The price of everything, in one place, and side by side.

Live · free, no account needed
{{icon:ChartLine}} Open OmniPrice {{icon:GitCompareArrows}} Try a comparison
  • Food
  • Wages
  • Energy
  • Commodities
  • Currencies
  • Crypto
  • Inflation
  • Stocks soon

{{icon:Layers}} Everything together

Groceries, pay, oil, wheat, currencies, crypto and inflation, on one board.

{{icon:GitCompareArrows}} Compare anything

Up to five series on one chart, each indexed to 100, whatever it is measured in.

{{icon:Clock}} Priced in work time

US groceries shown as minutes of work at the average wage, now and a decade ago.

{{icon:Landmark}} Official sources

ONS, US Bureau of Labor Statistics, IMF, ECB and EIA data, credited on every chart.

{{icon:History}} Decades of history

From the last month back to 1947, with high, low and average for any range.

{{icon:ShieldCheck}} Private by design

No account, no cookies and no tracking. Data is fetched by our server, not your browser.

For information only, not financial advice. See the OmniPrice terms and privacy notes.

PROJECT 03

Homebase

A beautiful start page that’s entirely yours, and entirely private.

Live · free · nothing leaves your browser
{{icon:House}} Open Homebase {{icon:Settings}} Set it as your home page
  • Event Horizon
  • Aurora
  • Synthwave
  • Liquid Gradient
  • Lava Lamp
  • Digital Rain
  • Sakura
  • Firefly Forest
  • Snowfall
  • Ocean Sunset
  • City Rain
  • Warp Speed
  • Paper
  • Terminal
  • Midnight
  • Your Photo

{{icon:Palette}} 16 living themes

GPU shaders and particle scenes, from a black hole to cherry blossom, plus your own photo.

{{icon:SlidersHorizontal}} Tune every detail

Each theme’s colours, speed, density and effects, plus fonts, glass, blur and corner roundness.

{{icon:LayoutGrid}} 20 widgets

Weather, to-dos, notes, focus timer, world clocks, calendar, currency, markets, moon phase and more.

{{icon:Move}} Drag, drop, resize

Arrange widgets however you like, in three sizes, with as many copies of each as you want.

{{icon:Gauge}} Smooth on any device

Sharp on high-res screens, adapts quality to your device, pauses when hidden and respects reduced motion.

{{icon:ShieldCheck}} Private by design

No account and no tracking. Your setup is stored only in your browser, with backups you control.

Guides for Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc and phones are built in. See the Homebase privacy notes.
