{{icon:Layers}} Everything together

Groceries, pay, oil, wheat, currencies, crypto and inflation, on one board.

PROJECT 05

Loopline

A drum machine and bass sequencer that lives in your browser.

Live · free · works offline once loaded
{{icon:AudioLines}} Make a beat
  • Kick
  • Snare
  • Clap
  • Hats
  • Tom
  • Bass synth
  • 4 scales
  • Swing

{{icon:Drum}} Tap to build a beat

Six drums and a bass line on a 16-step grid. Press Play and edit while it loops.

{{icon:AudioLines}} Every sound is synthesised

Kicks, snares and saw bass made live with the Web Audio API. No samples, no downloads.

{{icon:Music}} Always in key

Pick a key and scale and the bass rows only offer notes that fit.

{{icon:Share2}} Share it as a link

The whole beat fits in the link. Whoever opens it hears exactly what you made.

{{icon:Download}} Export a WAV

Render four loops to a WAV file to drop into a video or another music app.

{{icon:ShieldCheck}} Private by design

No account and nothing uploaded. Your last beat is kept in your browser.

Runs entirely in your browser. See the privacy notes.

PROJECT 07

Orbit Lab

A gravity sandbox. Fling planets and watch physics happen.

Live · free · real N-body physics
{{icon:Orbit}} Launch a planet
  • Solar system
  • Binary stars
  • Figure eight
  • Galaxy collision
  • Empty space

{{icon:Rocket}} Slingshot launches

Press, pull back and let go. Pull further to throw harder, and see where it will fly.

{{icon:Orbit}} Real gravity

Every body pulls on every other, with a stable integrator, so orbits keep going.

{{icon:Atom}} Collisions merge

Bodies that touch combine, keeping their mass and momentum. Throw a star into a planet.

{{icon:Sparkles}} Five scenes

A solar system, binary stars, the famous figure-eight three-body orbit and a galaxy collision.

{{icon:Gauge}} You control time

Pause, slow down to a quarter or speed up to four times, with trails to show the paths.

{{icon:Hand}} Made for touch

Works with a mouse or a finger, and the camera follows the action.

Runs entirely in your browser. See the privacy notes.
